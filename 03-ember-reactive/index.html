How we cook

Marinate overnight.

Lamb and chicken sit in yoghurt, garlic, lemon and our spice mix for at least 12 hours. It's why the meat stays juicy after the coals.

Char on real coals.

No gas, no flat-top. The grill is lit at 10am and fed all day. Smoke is the seasoning.

Serve in minutes.

Rice, salad, warm bread, house sauces. Into the box and out the door, into your hands or onto a delivery bike, while it's still hissing.
