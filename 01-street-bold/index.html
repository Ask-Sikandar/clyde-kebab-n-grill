About us

A family grill, right here in Clyde.

We're a family-run kebab shop in the heart of Clyde. Everything on the menu is the food we grew up on: skewers over real coals, rice cooked in stock, bread you tear with your hands.

No shortcuts, no frozen meat, no microwave. If it's not good enough for our own table, it doesn't go out the pass.
